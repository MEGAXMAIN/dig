Dig turns Mermaid flowcharts into native, movable shapes, connectors, labels, and phase containers on a ClickUp Whiteboard.

If your bookmarks bar is hidden, show it with ⌘⇧B on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows.

Use Dig

Prepare Mermaid syntaxGenerate it from a transcript, a workflow summary, or open an existing .mermaid file.

Open the target ClickUp WhiteboardPlace the canvas roughly where you want the diagram to appear.

Click the Dig bookmarkPaste the Mermaid source, preview it, and drag phase headers if needed.

Inject editable shapesIf your browser blocks automatic injection, Dig leaves the native-shape payload on your clipboard—click the canvas and press ⌘V or Ctrl+V.

Important: Dig uses ClickUp Whiteboard’s clipboard compatibility rather than an official Whiteboard API. ClickUp changes can affect it. Test on a spare board before a customer session.
